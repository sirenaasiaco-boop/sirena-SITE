002 / About Us

Who
We Are

Sirena Asia is a community of elite professional mermaids and underwater performers, born in Asia and performing worldwide, with teams from Taiwan to the UAE. Our Elites are certified mermaid instructors and rescue-trained freedivers, with years of aquarium shows behind them.

We are not an agency. Each mermaid performs and earns in her own name; Sirena brings them together, shares the work and gives their talent a bigger stage. Need a team? We build it around your show.

Our Services

What We Do

✦

Aquarium & Resort Shows

Choreographed underwater shows for aquariums, resorts and pools, by the season or the night. No tank? Our tank partner can bring one in.

⊕

Brand Activations

Launches and campaigns built around an underwater mermaid moment people film and share.

◈

Private & Luxury Events

Galas, weddings and private parties with a tank or pool, styled to your theme.

◇

Expos & Conferences

Show-stopping tank appearances that pull a crowd to your booth.

◎

Mermaid Team Setup & Training

Casting, SOPs, training and daily show running for venues starting a mermaid programme.

✧

PADI Mermaid Courses

From Discover Mermaid for first-timers to instructor-led certification, kids and adults.
